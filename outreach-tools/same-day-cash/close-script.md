# Phone / door close script

## Opener (10 seconds)

"Hi, it's Hameed from Ironsight — I built you a quick demo for [business]. Have you got 60 seconds?"

## Value (20 seconds)

- Website: "People find you on Google but can't book without ringing. I can have a simple site live this week."
- QuoteGenie: "Customers type the job and get a priced estimate from your rates, even when you're on a job."
- ReviewBoost: "When a job is done, they get a text asking for a Google review. Reminder follows if they forget."

## Ask (deposit)

"Christmas bookings are picking up. If you put **£[97/147/199]** today I start this week. Card, bank transfer, or PayPal. Rest when it's live."

## Handle objections

| They say | You say |
| --- | --- |
| "Too expensive" | "It's a one-off. The deposit only covers starting this week — full price is £[197/297/499]." |
| "Think about it" | "Totally fair. I only have two Christmas slots left. Want me to hold one until 6pm with a deposit?" |
| "Send info" | "I already did — here's the demo again. Easiest is a 10-minute look now while I'm here / on the phone." |
| "No website budget" | "Then do ReviewBoost for £97 deposit — more reviews, no full rebuild." |

## After yes

1. Send payment link in WhatsApp/SMS immediately.
2. Confirm amount + what they get in one message.
3. Ask for logo, colours, Google review link / price list.
4. Schedule go-live date this week.
